Nicole Biscaro

I am Nicole Biscaro, holding a Bachelor's degree in Modern Literature and currently pursuing a Master's degree in Digital and Public Humanities, both at Ca' Foscari University in Venice. Passionate about literature, cinema, and data visualization, I aspire to bridge the gap between the humanities and digital tools. With this project, I aim to explore innovative ways to analyze a complex text like *To the Lighthouse* using digital methodologies, bringing new insights into its structure and themes.
