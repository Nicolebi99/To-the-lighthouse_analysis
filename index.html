The Hogarth Press, London, 1927

Virginia Woolf (1882-1941) was an influential English modernist author. She is best known for her novels, essays, and short stories that explore complex themes of consciousness and time. Together with her husband Leonard Woolf, she founded the Hogarth Press, a publishing house that was instrumental in the success of several notable authors of the time.
